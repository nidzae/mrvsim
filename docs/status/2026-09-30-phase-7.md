# Phase 7 status — Policy engine and optimizer (TDD §8)

Date: 2026-09-30

## What was built

`mrvsim.policy`:

- **`policy.py`** — `SensorPolicy` (enabled, coverage, coverage basis, frequency per year, targeting), `Rule` (three kinds: `satellite_detect_to_aircraft` with X and N; `cms_run_length_to_drone` with H and N; `budget_to_widest_interval` with a monthly budget; plus per-facility cooldown and cap), `Policy` (sensors, rules, validation-tier filter) with validation, YAML save/load, and conversion to/from the `policy` config section used by the simulator and pipeline.
- **`rules.py`** — daily evaluation of trigger rules on the triggering sensors' logs; `simulate_with_rules` (two-pass: triggers → cue → full simulation); `allocate_budget_to_widest` (monthly fast-estimator pass, budget to the widest intervals).
- **`optimize.py`** — `SearchSpace`, Optuna NSGA-II study over (cost, width) with constraints κ ≥ κ_min and certified throughput share ≥ θ; reduced-R trials; `pareto_front`; re-scoring of the top-k at full R; `ParetoResult.to_dict()` for the API.

## Tests (7 new; all pass)

- Policy YAML round trip and config-section bridge; validation errors (coverage, unknown rule target, frequency range).
- **Satellite-detect rule**: cued aircraft visits land 1–N days after each trigger, respect the per-facility cap, appear as exactly those (facility, hour) rows in the final observation set, and the trigger detections are reproduced in the full pass (named seed streams).
- **CMS run-length rule**: cues drone surveys after ≥ H detected hours, respects the cooldown, produces per-source survey rows.
- **Widest-interval budget**: 12 months × budget visits, cap respected, visits present in the final set.
- Pareto-front logic (dominance, infeasible excluded).
- Optuna study (slow): 4 trials, feasible frontier, top-k re-scored, serialisable.
