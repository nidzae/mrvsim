# Phase 5 status — Scoring (TDD §7)

Date: 2026-09-30

## What was built

`mrvsim.score`:

- **`metrics.py`** — `Bar` (from the `scoring` config: `bar_mass_t_yr`, `bar_intensity`, `w_max`); `classify()` three-state rule (certified / fails / indeterminate); `score_replication()` computing per KPI: calibration (with binomial SE), median relative half-width, median bias, certified / fails / indeterminate shares by facility count and stratum-weighted by count and by throughput, certified MMBtu; per-stratum calibration; `completeness_from_detection_probability()` (PRD §5.5) overall and per basin.
- **`completeness.py`** — `detection_probability_once()`: P̄_j over the realised usable snapshot opportunities, survey visits, and CMS hours, with each opportunity's wind and surface factors.
- **`cost.py`** — `deployment_cost()` from the sensor YAML cost fields by schedule type; `cost_metrics()` (total, per tonne detected, per certified MMBtu); `detected_mass_kg()`.
- **`aggregate.py`** — `Metric(mean, se, n)` and `ScoreReport` with Monte Carlo SEs over replications, per-stratum calibration means, state shares, `calibration_ok()`.

`mrvsim.pipeline`:

- **`run_replication(cfg, seeds, rep, …)`** — population → deployment plan → observations → inputs → fast estimator → scores for one replication; optional `facilities_per_stratum` subsample and `n_draws` override for interactive use.
- **`run_scored(cfg, …)`** — R replications under a `RunContext`, aggregation, `runs/<id>/summary.json` (headline metrics with SEs, completeness by basin, per-stratum calibration, state shares, citation keys, provenance flags, `calibration_ok`), plus arrays of posteriors / truth / states and the last replication's population and observations for drill-down.

## Tests (114/114; 5 new)

- Three-state classification exhaustive on hand-built posteriors (certified, too wide, fails, straddles, certified).
- Calibration, width, bias, and weighted shares on synthetic posteriors; aggregation over two identical replications gives SE 0.
- Completeness definition and bounds (only sources > 10 kg/h; per-basin values).
- **Cost model reads per-sensor costs from the YAML**: aircraft visits × per-visit, CMS facilities × per-site-year, GHGSat taskings × per-tasking; totals add up; completeness in [0, 1]; subsample scored count.
- `run_scored` persists `summary.json`, population, observations; aggregates over R = 2 with finite SEs; byte-reproducible across two runs (slow).
