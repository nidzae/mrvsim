# Phase 4 status — Estimator (TDD §6)

Date: 2026-09-30

## What was built

`mrvsim.estimate`:

- **`inputs.py`** — `build_inputs(pop, obs, library, seeds)` assembles what the estimator may see: stratum, surface reflectance, f_gas, the noisy denominator Ĝ (σ_G = 0.05 GHGRP / 0.15 otherwise, TDD §6.6), snapshot non-detections grouped by (facility, sensor, 0.5 m/s wind bin), individual detections, per-visit survey detections, and CMS run-length summaries. Oracle columns are never read.
- **`likelihood.py`** — exact 2^K state enumeration grouped by drawn K (TDD §6.3); snapshot non-detection and detection terms with the false-positive mixture (§6.3, §4.4); per-source survey term with Poisson-process association (§6.5); CMS composite likelihood on detected fraction, mean detected-run length (OR-over-sources hazard, usable-hour splicing, false-call runs), and mean log reported rate (moment-matched log of the detected sum) (§6.4 v1); denominator draws (§6.6).
- **`fast.py`** — prior draws reusing the population generator's samplers (empirical Bayes, §6.2); `make_facility_loglik`; `estimate_facility` with `method = "is" | "smc" | "auto"`; `run_fast_estimator` → `PosteriorSummary` with p5/p10/p50/p90/p95 of mass and intensity, posterior on mean duty cycle, ESS, `covers()`, `relative_half_width()`.
- **`smc.py`** — tempered sequential Monte Carlo with adaptive temperature ladder, systematic resampling, and two rejuvenation moves (random walk on ln q and ν; prior-independence redraw of a source or of K). Used automatically when importance-sampling ESS < 200.
- **`exact.py`** — PyMC model for one facility (Categorical K, Bernoulli z, Normal ln q and ν, Normal ln G; 2^K_max enumeration as a `Potential`; compound NUTS + Gibbs steps), returning the same percentiles.
- **`budget.py`** — variance budget by oracle ablation (TDD §6.8) with the six components; the spatial-completeness and false-call ablations re-simulate observations with the new `force_usable` / `no_false_positives` switches in the simulator.

## Acceptance tests (CLAUDE.md Phase 4) — all pass

- **One steady source, 20 aircraft passes:** median |relative error| of the posterior median = 5 %; the 90 % interval covers truth on 88 % of 24 facilities; ESS ≥ 49 without SMC.
- **Intermittent source, no CMS vs CMS (PRD §7.4 identifiability):** 90 % interval on the facility mean duty cycle has width 0.96 without CMS (the prior) and 0.05 with CMS; the mass interval narrows from w = 2.5 to 1.1. The CMS posterior on *mean π of included sources* centres below the single-source truth (0.05 vs 0.076) because multi-source draws explain the same detected fraction with several smaller duty cycles; the mass posterior is unbiased.
- Exact PyMC path agrees with the fast path on the steady-source facility (medians within 15 %; truth covered).
- Variance budget shares sum to one; temporal-sampling oracle narrows the interval for an intermittent source.
- Enumeration matches brute force; no observations returns the prior; byte-reproducible.
- **Calibration mechanics (new, slow):** default-like policy on 210 facilities: coverage of mass and intensity in [0.82, 0.97], |bias| < 0.15.

Measured on the default population (6,300 facilities, 2 aircraft passes, monthly GHGSat on top 30 %, TROPOMI, CMS on top 20 %; one replication, 315-facility subsample): **coverage 0.886 (mass), 0.876 (intensity)**; misses split 5.7 % below / 5.7 % above; median bias 0.00; CMS-only policy 0.905 (bias −0.10).

## Three spec problems found and fixed (all logged)

1. **[p10, p90] is an 80 % interval.** TDD §6.7 and PRD §5.4 defined the reported interval by the 10th and 90th percentiles while §7 calls it a 90 % interval with κ ∈ [0.85, 0.95]. A prior-only run (no sensors, posterior = prior) gave κ = 0.78 ≈ 0.80, which exposed it. Now: two-sided 90 % interval [p5, p95] for calibration and width; one-sided p90 kept as the certification bound K̂_U,90 (PRD §5.3 unchanged). PRD §5.4 formula marked superseded and amended.
2. **Estimand mismatch.** TDD §6.7's M̂ = Σ ω π q T is the process expectation, but truth and calibration use the realised state path. The posterior is now the predictive of realised mass with renewal-reward variance per source (lognormal, right-skewed). `realised=False` recovers the TDD expression.
3. **Duty cycles were not latent.** With stratum-level ν the generator gave every intermittent source in a stratum the same π. Added `sigma_nu_on/off` (PLACEHOLDER 0.7) per stratum so π varies across sources, in both generator and prior.

Plus two implementation bugs caught by the sensor-by-sensor coverage diagnostic: the CMS rate term compared the *sum* of detected reported rates to a detectability-weighted *mean* (+15 % bias), and the run-length predictor averaged per-source miss rates instead of the facility OR (made side sources look impossible). Both fixed; see DECISION_LOG.

## Performance (honest)

| Setting | Per facility | 6,300 facilities |
|---|---|---|
| 10⁴ draws, auto IS/SMC | 50–110 ms | 340–700 s |
| 4,000 draws, auto | ~65 ms | ~410 s |
| 2,000 draws, IS only | ~15 ms | ~95 s |

TDD §10 budgeted 20 s for the fast path and PRD N3 requires < 60 s for interactive recompute at R = 20. **Neither is met by this NumPy implementation.** Options, in the order I would try them: (a) numba kernels for the enumeration and snapshot terms (numba 0.65.1 installs and runs against numpy 2.4.6 here; 11 cores available) — expected 5–10×; (b) multiprocessing across facilities — ~8×; (c) interactive mode on a stratified subsample (e.g. 30 facilities per stratum) with 2,000 draws and R = 5, scaled by stratum weights; (d) caching posteriors per (facility, policy) and re-estimating only facilities whose observation log changed. (a)+(b)+(c) together reach the N3 target; none changes the statistical method. Decision needed from Nidhi on whether N3 may be met via subsampling (c), which changes what "full recompute" means.
