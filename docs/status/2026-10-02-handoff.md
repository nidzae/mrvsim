## State of the model and tool (what changed 2026-10-01/02)

1. **Certification rule (PRD §5.3, §5.4, §5.4a; DECISION_LOG "certification is the compliance decision at 95 %").** certified iff p95 ≤ bar; fails iff p5 > bar; indeterminate iff the 90 % interval straddles it. Width w = (p95−p5)/(2·median) is a precision grade (precise iff w ≤ w_max), shown as a dark ring on the map; it no longer blocks certification but is still the optimizer objective. Evidence = posterior ln(p95/p5) over the prior's; "prior-only" (faded dot) when ratio > 0.9 or no usable observation. The estimator persists a 101-point posterior quantile grid, prior p05/p50/p95 and evidence counts per facility, so the drill-down states P(K ≤ B | data) directly.
2. **Sensor assignment visible (drill-down "Monitoring at this facility").** Per sensor: rule, selected or not and why (throughput rank), planned visit days, incidental looks. `throughput` targeting is a top-k cutoff (labels corrected from "throughput-weighted").
3. **Regional flight campaigns (DECISION_LOG "Regional flight campaigns").** Campaign/survey sensors take `scheduling: independent | campaign` and `campaign_days`; under campaign each basin is flown in one window per slice of the year. UI default sets campaign for Bridger.
4. **Incidental capture and field of regard (DECISION_LOG "Incidental capture").** Tasked satellites can be tasked on any pass within swath/2 plus the pointing reach (GHGSat-C: ±15° from the TLE-derived ~457 km → ~260 km band, ~40 daylight opportunities per site-year instead of ~3). Each tasked scene (GHGSat 12 × 12 km, aligned with ground-track heading) and each aircraft survey block (Bridger 0.5 km, an assumption) logs the facilities inside it as incidental snapshots sharing the target's cloud state; free of charge; feed likelihood, evidence and completeness. Policy flag `incidental_capture` (default on). Sensor YAMLs carry `orbit.fov_along_km/fov_cross_km/max_off_nadir_deg` and `footprint.survey_block_km`.
5. **UI.** Both KPIs in the panel (selected first, other at its own bar); hover tooltip on dots with both KPIs; resizable panel (drag left edge; width remembered in the browser); distinct timeline colours with hollow markers for incidental looks; probability bounds ("< 5 %", "> 95 %") for runs scored before the grid; "incidental capture" checkbox and scheduling controls under Sensors.
6. **Bugs fixed.** Blank screen on facility click (a reference error in the KPI block; reproduced with a headless jsdom render, `web/package.json` dev dependency); facility endpoint returned NaN JSON for unscored facilities (now nulls + `scored: false`).
