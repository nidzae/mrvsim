# Phase 3 status — Observation simulator (TDD §5)

Date: 2026-09-30

## What was built

`mrvsim.observe`:

- **`overpass.py`** — Skyfield propagation of pinned TLEs (`data/fitted/tle/`, CelesTrak snapshot 2026-09-30 with provenance) over the simulated year; swath test by point-to-segment cross-track distance with a KD-tree coarse search; adaptive step (20 s for narrow swaths, up to 180 s for TROPOMI); one opportunity per pass; solar zenith at pass time. **Cached per satellite per year** in `data/cache/overpasses/` with a key over TLE text, coordinates, swath, and step.
- **`solar.py`** — vectorised NOAA solar-position algorithm (no ephemeris download) [noaa-solar].
- **`deployment.py`** — `DeploymentPlan` built from the `policy` config section: coverage (facility or throughput basis), targeting (random / throughput), campaign frequency with stratified daytime visit hours, tasking frequency for tasked satellites. Tip-and-cue and adaptive targeting are Phase 7.
- **`simulator.py`** — gates exactly as TDD §5.2 with the conventions recorded in DECISION_LOG (cloud Bernoulli, aircraft cloud tolerance, SZA ≤ 70°, Weibull wind draw reused in the POD wind term, CMS outage and wind-sector Bernoullis); detection and reporting per TDD §5.3 with false positives per §4.4; site-level snapshots (`Q_i(t)`), per-source surveys, dense hourly CMS series OR-ed to a facility flag. `ObservationLog` (struct of arrays, non-detections and cloud-outs logged with conditions, oracle truth columns kept separate for scoring), `CMSLog` (bit-packed on disk), `ObservationSet` with save/load.

Default population, 7 sensors (2 aircraft passes everywhere, monthly GHGSat tasking on the top 30 % by throughput, TROPOMI + Sentinel-2A + Landsat 9 wall-to-wall, CMS on the top 20 %, annual OGI on 30 %): **9.0 s per replication** with cached overpasses (TDD §10 budget ≈ 10 s); first run 105 s to compute and cache five satellites' overpasses.

## Acceptance tests (all pass; 101/101 total, 17 new)

- **10 t/h steady source detected by TROPOMI on > 90 % of clear overpasses** (after the POD adjustment below).
- **5 kg/h source never truly detected by any of the seven satellites**; false positives are the separate λ_FP process and are flagged in the oracle column.
- **CMS log has 8,760 rows per instrumented facility**; usable share equals (1 − p_outage)·wind_sector_coverage; zero-rate hours rarely flagged, large-rate hours mostly detected.
- Overpass counts per facility-year match orbital geometry (TROPOMI ≈ 820, Sentinel-2A ≈ 90, Landsat 9 ≈ 55 counting both day and night sides); passes separated by > 10 min; cross-track ≤ swath/2; cache round trip.
- Solar zenith sanity (solstice noon, midnight, Permian January noon).
- Full multi-sensor simulation byte-reproducible for the same seed, different for a different seed; tasked satellite ≤ 12 looks per facility; aircraft exactly 2 visits per covered facility; OGI rows per source; save/load round trip.
